# Knowledge Base

This directory holds the research-writing standards that the GobeshonaLabAgent skills enforce.

The guidance here is a curated statement of **widely-accepted, general research-writing and
research-integrity principles** — the kind taught in graduate research-methods courses and codified
in common author guidelines, reporting standards, and publication-ethics norms across disciplines.
It is written in the project's own words as original guidance. It does **not** reproduce or
paraphrase any single proprietary or unpublished source, and it makes no claim to originate from a
specific book, author, or copyrighted text.

Contents:

- [`principles/research-writing-principles.md`](./principles/research-writing-principles.md) —
  cross-cutting principles for framing, writing, methodology, results, figures, references, ethics,
  clarity, and publication.
- [`quality-framework/manuscript-checklist.md`](./quality-framework/manuscript-checklist.md) —
  a section-by-section checklist, marked for automated vs. human review.
- [`quality-framework/review-rubric.md`](./quality-framework/review-rubric.md) —
  the scored rubric used by the `final-reviewer` skill.

Where a principle reflects an established community standard (for example IMRaD structure, CONSORT/
PRISMA-style reporting discipline, or COPE-style publication-ethics norms), that is noted generically
rather than attributed to any single text.
